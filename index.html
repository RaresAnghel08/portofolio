02 / Research · CCIR, 2026

Does socioeconomic class change how LLMs screen resumes?

A conjoint experiment on 10,000+ synthetic resumes across three models, run at the Cambridge Centre for International Research. Supervised by Dr. Danny Ebanks (Harvard IQSS) and Dominic Skinnion (MIT), and presented at the CCIR Student Research Symposium, King's College, Cambridge, in July 2026.

  • Claude Sonnet 4.6
    Significant class bias

    Observed for Data Analyst roles.

  • Gemini 3.5 Flash
    Near-zero bias

    Class had almost no effect on screening.

  • Qwen 2.5 (7B)
    Selective bias

    Bias appeared in some cases and not others.
